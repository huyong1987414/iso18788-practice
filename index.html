Exam A — 40 questions, 60 minutes

1. What is the full title of ISO 18788:2015?
A. Security and resilience — Security management systems for private security providers — Requirements
B. Private security operations — Code of practice for armed and unarmed guarding services
C. Management system for private security operations — Requirements with guidance for use
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Title / Clause 1 (Scope)
ISO 18788 is a requirements standard (auditable, certifiable) that also contains guidance (Annex A) for use. Supply-chain security is ISO 28000; business continuity is ISO 22301.
2. ISO 18788 is described as specifically applicable to organizations operating in which type of environment?
A. Where governance may be weak or the rule of law undermined
B. Only in stable jurisdictions with a mature guard-licensing regime
C. Only in States formally engaged in an international armed conflict
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1
The standard targets complex environments with weak governance or undermined rule of law (e.g. post-conflict areas). A note states it is not intended to add burdens on general guarding outside such circumstances. Maritime PSCs are covered by ISO 28007.
3. Which set of documents does ISO 18788 explicitly draw on and help organizations demonstrate conformance with?
A. The Geneva Conventions, the NATO SOFA model and ISO 9001
B. The UN Charter, the Arms Trade Treaty and ISO 31000
C. The Montreux Document, the ICoC and the UN Guiding Principles
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Introduction 0.1
The Introduction lists the Montreux Document (2008), the ICoC (2010) and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (2011); it also refers to the Voluntary Principles on Security and Human Rights.
4. To whom is the Montreux Document primarily addressed?
A. Individual security guards, as a personal code of behaviour
B. Certification bodies auditing private security companies
C. States, regarding PMSC operations during armed conflict
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Introduction 0.1 (Note 3)
The Montreux Document restates international legal obligations and good practices for States (contracting, territorial and home States). ISO 18788 notes it is addressed to States and armed conflict but is also instructive in similar conditions and for other entities.
5. Which management approach does ISO 18788 use as the basis of the SOMS?
A. Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)
B. Define-Measure-Analyse-Improve-Control (DMAIC)
C. Observe-Orient-Decide-Act (OODA)
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1 / Annex D
The Introduction states that the standard uses a Plan-Do-Check-Act approach; Annex D describes the management systems approach.
6. In ISO 18788, what does the verbal form “shall” indicate?
A. An auditable requirement
B. A recommendation
C. A permission
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1 (verbal forms)
“shall” = requirement; “should” = recommendation; “may” = permission; “can” = possibility or capability.
7. Which clause requires the organization to determine the boundaries and applicability of its SOMS, including documenting any exclusions and their justification?
A. 6.2 Security operations objectives and planning to achieve them
B. 5.3 Organization roles, responsibilities and authorities
C. 4.3 Determining the scope of the SOMS
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 4.3
Scope is determined in 4.3. In ISO 18788 the scope work also involves a Statement of Applicability for relevant Annex A guidance, based on risk and human rights impact analysis.
8. A PSC in Iraq wants to identify all subcontractors (local guard companies, vehicle hire, interpreters) involved in delivering its services and understand their risks. Which subclause specifically addresses this?
A. 4.1.4 Supply chain and subcontractor mapping and analysis
B. 6.1.3 Internal and external risk communication and consultation
C. 9.1.2 Evaluation of compliance
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 4.1.4
4.1.4 requires mapping and analysis of the supply chain and subcontractors as part of understanding context. Contractual control of subcontractors is further addressed in 7.1.2.4.
9. Top management must develop, document and publish a commitment to respect human rights and to conform with the ICoC and the Montreux Document. Where is this required?
A. 8.2 Establishing norms of behaviour and codes of ethical conduct
B. 7.4.2 Operational communications
C. 5.1.2 Statement of Conformance
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 5.1.2
5.1.2 is specific to ISO 18788 (not in the generic Annex SL text): top management publishes a Statement of Conformance covering human rights, the ICoC, the Montreux Document and the UN Guiding Principles.
10. Your company must keep an up-to-date register of Iraqi Ministry of Interior licensing rules for PSCs, weapons permits, client contract clauses and the ICoC. Which subclause primarily covers this?
A. 7.5.3 Control of documented information
B. 6.1.2 Legal and other requirements
C. 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of stakeholders
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 6.1.2
6.1.2 requires identifying, accessing and determining how legal and other requirements (laws, licences, contracts, voluntary commitments such as the ICoC) apply. Compliance with them is later evaluated under 9.1.2.
11. During risk assessment for a Baghdad–Basra convoy route, the results of risk analysis are compared with the organization's risk criteria to decide which risks need treatment. What is this step called?
A. Risk evaluation
B. Risk communication
C. Risk identification
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 4.1.5 / 6.1 (risk assessment)
Risk assessment = identification, analysis and evaluation (ISO 31000 terminology used by ISO 18788). Evaluation compares analysis results against the risk criteria defined under 4.1.5.
12. According to ISO 18788, the use of force by security personnel must be:
A. Sufficient to deter any future attack on the convoy
B. Approved in advance by the client for each incident
C. Reasonably necessary, proportional and lawful
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Introduction 0.2 / 8.3
The standard (Introduction 0.2 and clause 8.3) requires that the use of force is reasonably necessary, proportional and lawful, consistent with the ICoC.
13. During a convoy escort near Nasiriyah, an unidentified car approaches fast from behind. Circumstances allow time to react. Consistent with a use-of-force continuum, what should the rear vehicle team do first?
A. Disable the vehicle by firing at its engine block straight away
B. Use graduated measures such as visual and audible warnings first
C. Fire warning shots immediately to show the convoy is armed
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.3.3
8.3.3 (use of force continuum) expects a graduated response: the least force necessary, escalating only as the threat escalates, with de-escalation where possible. Warning shots are generally discouraged/prohibited in many rules for the use of force.
14. Consistent with ISO 18788 and the ICoC, when may security personnel use firearms (lethal force) against persons?
A. To protect any client property, including vehicles and fuel stocks
B. Whenever a driver refuses an instruction to stop at a checkpoint
C. Only to protect life against an imminent threat of death or serious injury
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 8.3.5
Lethal force (8.3.5) is a last resort limited to protecting life, in line with ICoC firearms principles. Property protection alone does not justify lethal force.
15. Guards at a static oilfield camp in Basra catch a local man stealing diesel. What is the appropriate action?
A. Hold him only as necessary and hand him to the police without delay
B. Detain him in a site container for several days as a deterrent
C. Question him at length to identify his network before reporting
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.4.1
8.4.1 (apprehension of persons): apprehension only where lawful and necessary, with respect for human rights and dignity, handing over to competent authorities without delay. Private security must not conduct prolonged detention or interrogation.
16. Gate guards at a Baghdad compound search visitors and their bags. Which subclause sets requirements for this activity?
A. 7.4.3 Risk communications
B. 8.4.2 Search
C. 8.5.2 Detention operations
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.4.2
8.4.2 covers searches of persons and property: carried out lawfully, with respect for dignity and cultural/gender sensitivities, under documented procedures.
17. A government client asks your company to guard persons held in custody. Which subclause addresses this type of service?
A. 8.4.1 Apprehension of persons
B. 8.5.2 Detention operations
C. 8.1.3 Respect for human rights
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.5.2
Detention operations are addressed in 8.5.2 under operations in support of law enforcement. Consistent with the ICoC, they are only undertaken where lawfully contracted by a State and with specifically trained personnel.
18. Before hiring 30 local guards for a site in Kirkuk, the company checks identity, criminal records, previous employment and any history of human rights abuse. Which subclause does this mainly support?
A. 8.6.2 Personnel
B. 10.2.2 Change management
C. 7.3 Awareness
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.6.2
Background screening and vetting of persons working on behalf of the organization are part of 8.6.2 (Personnel), consistent with ICoC vetting commitments.
19. An audit finds that the armoury at your site keeps no record of rifle issue/return and ammunition counts. Which subclause is most directly not met?
A. 8.6.3 Procurement and management of weapons, hazardous materials and munitions
B. 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of stakeholders
C. 8.6.4 Uniforms and markings
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.6.3
8.6.3 requires controlled procurement, storage, issue, accountability and disposal of weapons, ammunition and hazardous materials, with records.
20. What is the main purpose of the requirements on uniforms and markings for security personnel?
A. To promote the client's brand and corporate image at the site
B. To make personnel resemble soldiers so that potential attackers are deterred
C. To make personnel identifiable and distinct from military or police forces
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 8.6.4
8.6.4: personnel should be identifiable and accountable; they must not be confused with public forces (a core ICoC principle). Exceptions are risk-based and documented.
21. A guard wants to report, confidentially, that his supervisor is selling ammunition, and fears retaliation. Which requirement protects this kind of report?
A. 8.3.2 Weapons authorization
B. 8.8.4 Whistle-blower policy
C. 6.2.2 Achieving security operations and risk treatment objectives
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.8.4 (and 7.4.5)
8.8.4 requires a whistle-blower policy allowing reports without fear of retaliation; 7.4.5 requires that this policy is communicated.
22. Villagers near your static site complain that guards block a road they use to reach their fields. What does ISO 18788 expect?
A. Refer the villagers to the Iraqi courts, as only courts can decide this
B. A fair, accessible grievance procedure that investigates and responds
C. Accept the complaint only if the client formally forwards it to you
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.8.3
8.8.3 requires internal and external complaint and grievance procedures that third parties (e.g. local communities) can use; 7.4.4 requires communicating these procedures.
23. A guard accidentally discharges his rifle while clearing it at the gate; nobody is injured. What should happen next?
A. Report it, investigate the cause, record it and take corrective action
B. Take no action, since nobody was injured and no damage was caused
C. Dismiss the guard immediately and close the matter without a record
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.8.2
8.8.2 requires incident monitoring, reporting and investigation, including weapon discharges and near-misses; findings feed corrective action (10.1).
24. The company translates its grievance procedure into Arabic and posts it at the camp gate and in the nearby village. Which subclause does this directly fulfil?
A. 8.2 Establishing norms of behaviour and codes of ethical conduct
B. 7.4.4 Communicating complaint and grievance procedures
C. 9.1.3 Exercises and testing
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 7.4.4
Communication of grievance procedures to internal and external stakeholders is 7.4.4; the procedure itself is in 8.8.3.
25. Every six months the company checks whether it actually complies with weapons licence conditions, labour law and contract clauses. Which subclause is this?
A. 9.1.2 Evaluation of compliance
B. 10.2.3 Opportunities for improvement
C. 6.1.2 Legal and other requirements
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 9.1.2
6.1.2 identifies the requirements; 9.1.2 periodically evaluates compliance with them and keeps records.
26. The operations manager runs a rehearsal of a convoy ambush and casualty evacuation (CASEVAC) drill with the ops room. Which subclause does this support?
A. 4.1.2 Internal context
B. 9.1.3 Exercises and testing
C. 8.3.2 Weapons authorization
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 9.1.3
9.1.3 requires exercises and tests to validate plans and procedures (e.g. emergency response) and the competence of people, with lessons learned recorded.
27. Which statement about internal audits of the SOMS is correct?
A. Done by each manager auditing their own area of work
B. Done at planned intervals by objective and impartial auditors
C. Optional once the organization has achieved certification
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 9.2
9.2 requires an internal audit programme at planned intervals; auditor selection must ensure objectivity and impartiality. Management review (9.3) is a separate requirement.
28. “Status of actions from previous management reviews” and “results of audits” are examples of:
A. Management review inputs
B. Security operations objectives
C. Management review outputs
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 9.3.2
These are typical inputs to management review (9.3.2). Outputs (9.3.3) are decisions and actions, e.g. improvement opportunities, changes and resource needs.
29. In management system standards such as ISO 18788, what is a “nonconformity”?
A. Any security incident involving the use of weapons
B. A risk that has been identified but not yet treated
C. Non-fulfilment of a requirement
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Clause 3 / 10.1
A nonconformity is the non-fulfilment of a requirement (Annex SL common term). Handling nonconformities is in 10.1.
30. A night-shift guard is found asleep at his post. The supervisor immediately replaces him. Investigation shows 16-hour shifts are common across the site. Which response represents corrective action?
A. Revising the shift roster to remove the fatigue cause
B. Replacing the sleeping guard with a fresh guard at once
C. Giving the sleeping guard a formal written warning
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 10.1
Replacing the guard is a correction (immediate fix). Corrective action (10.1) eliminates the cause to prevent recurrence and its effectiveness is reviewed.
31. A client changes your contract from static site guarding to daily mobile escorts between Erbil and Mosul. Which subclause most directly requires these changes to be managed in a planned way?
A. 10.2.2 Change management
B. 7.5.3 Control of documented information
C. 9.3.2 Review input
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 10.2.2
ISO 18788 includes a specific change management subclause (10.2.2): changes affecting operations are assessed for risk and managed before implementation.
32. In July, guards at a Basra site work outdoors in 50 °C heat. The company provides shaded posts, water, rotation and heat-stress training. Which clause does this mainly address?
A. 8.1.3 Respect for human rights
B. 8.7 Occupational health and safety
C. 7.1.2 Structural requirements
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.7
8.7 requires the organization to protect the health, safety and welfare of persons working on its behalf, based on hazard and risk assessment.
33. Which informative annex of ISO 18788 helps an organization get started by comparing current practice with the standard (gap analysis)?
A. Annex A
B. Annex C
C. Annex E
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause Annex C
Annex A: guidance on use; Annex B: general principles; Annex C: getting started — gap analysis; Annex D: management systems approach; Annex E: qualifiers to application.
34. Analysing Iraq's political situation, tribal dynamics, security threats and the national legal environment is part of understanding which aspect of context?
A. Internal context (4.1.2)
B. Policy (5.2)
C. External context (4.1.3)
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 4.1.3
Political, legal, social, cultural and threat factors outside the organization belong to the external context (4.1.3). Internal context (4.1.2) covers the organization's own structure, culture, capabilities and resources.
35. Clause 4.2 of ISO 18788 requires the organization to understand the needs and expectations of which group?
A. The certification body and its auditors only
B. Shareholders and investors of the organization only
C. Stakeholders, such as clients, communities and authorities
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 4.2
ISO 18788 uses the term “stakeholders” in 4.2 (other Annex SL standards say “interested parties”). Amendment 1:2024 adds a note that relevant stakeholders can have climate-change-related requirements.
36. Old versions of the convoy standard operating procedure (SOP) are still being used in some vehicles. Which subclause is most directly not met?
A. 9.1.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation
B. 7.5.3 Control of documented information
C. 7.2.3 Training and competence evaluation

Answer: B · Clause 7.5.3
7.5.3 requires documented information to be available where needed, protected, and under version control, preventing unintended use of obsolete documents.
37. Which is an awareness requirement for persons working under the organization's control?
A. Awareness of the policy, their role and the effects of not conforming
B. Personal sign-off on the client contract and its schedules
C. A university degree in security or risk management from a recognised school
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 7.3
7.3 (Awareness) covers policy, contribution to SOMS effectiveness and the implications of not conforming — including human rights and use-of-force rules.
38. Your company defines radio call signs, check-in intervals, and the reporting chain between convoy vehicles and the ops room. Which subclause addresses this?
A. 8.2 Establishing norms of behaviour and codes of ethical conduct
B. 6.1.3 Internal and external risk communication and consultation
C. 7.4.2 Operational communications
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 7.4.2
Communication needed to run operations (command, control and reporting) is under 7.4.2. Risk communication with stakeholders is under 6.1.3/7.4.3.
39. Which is the best example of a security operations objective under clause 6.2?
A. The company will follow ISO 18788 and avoid all incidents forever
B. All staff should always try their best to be safe at work every day
C. All armed guards complete use-of-force refresher training by 30 June

Answer: C · Clause 6.2
6.2 requires objectives that are consistent with the policy, measurable (if practicable), monitored and communicated, with plans stating what, who, resources, when and how evaluated.
40. While writing procedures for preventing and responding to undesirable or disruptive events (e.g. a protest blocking the site gate), which consideration does ISO 18788 treat as the first priority?
A. Recovering the cost of the disruption from the client
B. Anticipation and prevention of undesirable events
C. Rapid dispersal of the crowd using proportionate force
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.1.4
8.1.4 lists considerations for such procedures, including safeguarding life, respect for human dignity, notifying appropriate authorities, minimizing adverse community impact and lessons learned — with anticipation and prevention as the first priority.

Exam B — 40 questions, 60 minutes

1. Which ISO technical committee is responsible for ISO 18788?
A. ISO/TC 283 Occupational health and safety management
B. ISO/TC 176 Quality management and quality assurance
C. ISO/TC 292 Security and resilience
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Foreword
The Foreword states that ISO/TC 292, Security and resilience, is responsible for the document (TC 292 also owns ISO 22301 and ISO 28000).
2. Which best describes the International Code of Conduct for Private Security Service Providers (ICoC)?
A. A voluntary code of human-rights-based principles for security providers
B. A binding international treaty negotiated and ratified by States only
C. An ISO management system standard for private security companies
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1
The ICoC (9 Nov 2010) is a voluntary code. A note in ISO 18788 explains it reflects the Montreux Document and the Guiding Principles. ISO 18788 provides an auditable way to show conformance with it.
3. The UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights operationalize the “Protect, Respect and Remedy” framework. What does “Respect” refer to?
A. The corporate responsibility to respect human rights
B. The State duty to protect against human rights abuse
C. Victims' access to judicial and non-judicial remedy
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1
Protect = State duty; Respect = corporate responsibility; Remedy = access to remedy (e.g. grievance mechanisms such as ISO 18788 clause 8.8.3).
4. A sentence in ISO 18788 uses “should”. How should an auditor interpret it?
A. A permission
B. A recommendation
C. An auditable requirement
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause Introduction 0.1 (verbal forms)
“should” indicates a recommendation (e.g. much of Annex A guidance). Only “shall” statements are auditable requirements.
5. For ISO 18788 purposes, “national laws” can include the laws of several countries. Which is NOT among those listed?
A. The country where the weapons were made
B. The country of the client
C. The country in which the operations take place
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1 (Note 4)
The Introduction note lists the countries of the organization, its personnel, the operations and the client. For a Chinese-owned PSC operating in Iraq for a foreign client, several legal systems may apply at once.
6. Competence, awareness, communication and documented information are grouped under which clause of ISO 18788?
A. Clause 6 Planning
B. Clause 7 Support
C. Clause 8 Operation
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause Clauses 4–10 (Annex SL structure)
Clause 7 (Support) covers 7.1 Resources, 7.2 Competence, 7.3 Awareness, 7.4 Communication and 7.5 Documented information.
7. In the PDCA cycle, the “Check” stage mainly corresponds to which clause?
A. Clause 8 Operation
B. Clause 10 Improvement
C. Clause 9 Performance evaluation
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause Clauses 9 / PDCA
Plan ≈ clauses 4–7 (context, leadership, planning, support); Do ≈ 8; Check ≈ 9 (monitoring, audit, management review); Act ≈ 10.
8. Your company is already certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 45001. What does ISO 18788 say about integration?
A. It can be integrated with them into one management system
B. It must run as a fully separate system with separate documents
C. It automatically replaces both standards once certified
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause Introduction 0.1
The Introduction states ISO 18788 is designed to be integrated with other management systems (quality, safety, resilience, environmental, information security, risk). The common Annex SL structure makes this easier.
9. Which subclause in clause 8 specifically requires operational controls ensuring respect for human rights during security operations?
A. 8.1.3 Respect for human rights
B. 8.1.2 Performance of security-related functions
C. 8.8.1 Incident management — General
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.1.3
8.1.3 addresses respect for human rights in operations; human rights also run through policy (5.2), the Statement of Conformance (5.1.2), risk assessment (6.1) and grievance handling (8.8.3).
10. A checkpoint guard accepts cash from drivers to let them pass without a vehicle search. Which requirement most directly addresses this behaviour?
A. 7.1.2.5 Financial and administrative procedures
B. 8.2 Norms of behaviour and codes of ethical conduct
C. 6.1.3 Risk communication and consultation
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.2
8.2 requires a documented Code of Ethics for all persons working on the organization's behalf (employees, subcontractors, outsource partners), covering issues such as bribery and corruption.
11. Your company subcontracts a local Iraqi guard force for an outer perimeter. Which statement is correct?
A. Only the price and duration need to be documented in the subcontract
B. Once subcontracted, the work falls outside your SOMS and your responsibility
C. A documented agreement binding them to the same obligations, controlled in your SOMS
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 7.1.2.4 (with 4.4 and 4.1.4)
4.4 requires subcontracted/outsourced activities within scope to be controlled in the SOMS; 7.1.2.4 requires a defined subcontracting process and documented agreements (human rights commitments, incident reporting, command and control limits, etc.).
12. The company plans to issue pepper spray and batons to unarmed gate guards. What must be in place first?
A. Only the client's verbal approval for issuing the items
B. Legal authorization, procedures and training in their use
C. Nothing further, because these items are less-lethal
Show answer
Answer: B · Clause 8.3.4
8.3.4 (less-lethal force) — less-lethal options are still force: they must be lawful, authorized, covered by procedures and used only by trained personnel (8.3.7).
13. A guard asks to carry a personal pistol on duty that is not on his Iraqi weapons card and on which he has never qualified. Which subclause is relevant?
A. 8.3.2 Weapons authorization
B. 8.3.4 Less-lethal force
C. 8.6.4 Uniforms and markings
Show answer
Answer: A · Clause 8.3.2
8.3.2 requires that personnel carry only weapons for which they are lawfully authorized and trained/qualified, under the organization's documented authorization process.
14. Local police ask your armed guards to help them forcibly disperse a protest outside the client's facility. Which subclause is most relevant?
A. 7.4.2 Operational communications
B. 8.8.3 Internal and external complaint and grievance procedures
C. 8.3.6 Use of force in support of law enforcement
Show answer
Answer: C · Clause 8.3.6
8.3.6 specifically covers force used when supporting law enforcement; it must be within legal authority, the contract and the rules for the use of force. Private security should not take on public-force roles without lawful basis.
15. Annual refresher training on rules for the use of force, de-escalation and human rights for all armed guards falls mainly under:
A. 8.3.7 Use of force training

Answer: A · Clause 8.3.7
8.3.7 requires use-of-force training (initial and refresher), supported by general competence requirements in 7.2.

8.5 covers operations in support of law enforcement: 8.5.1 law enforcement support and 8.5.2 detention operations.
