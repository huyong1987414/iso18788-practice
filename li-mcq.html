Exam A — 80 questions, 3 hours

1. The CEO of a PSC tells a prospective client: "Once we are ISO 18788 certified, you have a guarantee that our guards will never violate human rights." How should the SOMS project lead correct this statement?
A. Certification only covers the documentation of the company and says nothing about field operations
B. The statement is accurate, because certified PSCs are legally exempt from human rights claims in Iraq
C. Certification shows a managed system to prevent, mitigate and remedy adverse impacts, not a guarantee
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: Introduction (0.1, 0.2)
ISO 18788 provides a risk-based management framework to prevent and mitigate undesirable events, including human rights impacts, and to report, investigate and remedy them when they occur. It cannot guarantee zero violations, gives no legal immunity, and it does cover operations (clause 8), not just documents.
2. A PSC is analysing escalation-of-force incident trends and conducting internal audits of its convoy operations. In the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle used by ISO 18788, which phase do these activities belong to?
A. Do
B. Act
C. Check
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: Introduction 0.3 / Annex D; Clause 9
Monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation and internal audit (clause 9) form the Check phase. Implementing operational controls is Do (clause 8); taking corrective action and improving is Act (clause 10).
3. A PSC is already a member of the International Code of Conduct Association (ICoCA). Its director asks why it should also implement ISO 18788. Which answer best describes the relationship?
A. ISO 18788 gives auditable management system requirements that help show how ICoC commitments are put into practice
B. The ICoC applies only to maritime security companies, whereas ISO 18788 applies only to land-based security operations in conflict areas
C. ISO 18788 replaces the ICoC, so the PSC can withdraw from the ICoCA once it has been certified
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: Introduction 0.1; 5.1.2
The ICoC sets out principles and standards of conduct; ISO 18788 provides a certifiable management system framework that helps organizations implement and demonstrate conformance with such commitments, together with the Montreux Document and UN Guiding Principles. It neither replaces the ICoC nor divides the land/maritime field this way.
4. A PSC in Basra is already certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 and now plans to implement ISO 18788. Which approach is generally most effective?
A. Postpone ISO 18788 until the two existing certificates are withdrawn, to avoid any duplication of effort
B. Build the SOMS as a fully separate system with its own document control, audits and management review
C. Integrate the SOMS with the existing systems, using the shared structure and common processes
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: Annex D; 4.4
ISO 18788 follows the common management system structure (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement), so common processes such as document control, internal audit, management review and corrective action can be shared. A separate parallel system creates duplication; withdrawing existing certificates is unnecessary.
5. A small PSC with limited resources wants to implement ISO 18788 progressively over three years, starting with its highest-risk armed convoy operations. Which part of ISO 18788 supports this approach?
A. The maturity model for phased implementation in the guidance annex
B. Clause 9.2, which lets each requirement be audited only once every three years
C. Clause 3, which allows small companies to ignore requirements until they grow larger
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: Annex A (A.11)
Annex A (A.11) describes a maturity model for phased implementation, allowing an organization to build its SOMS step by step. Clause 3 contains only terms and definitions, and the internal audit clause does not allow requirements to be ignored. For certification, all applicable requirements must eventually be met.
6. An oil company asks a PSC to show where, in its management system, top management formally commits to respecting human rights and to conforming with the Montreux Document and the ICoC. Which ISO 18788 requirement most directly provides this evidence?
A. The list of monitoring and measurement methods defined for performance evaluation
B. The statement of conformance required under leadership and commitment
C. The records of competence evaluation that are kept for each individual guard
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 5.1.2
Clause 5.1.2 requires a statement of conformance in which the organization commits to applicable legal obligations and voluntary commitments, such as the Montreux Document, the ICoC and human rights principles. Competence records and monitoring methods do not show a top-level commitment.
7. A PSC's draft security operations policy commits the company to "high quality service and profitable growth" and contains nothing else. Which change is needed to meet ISO 18788?
A. Keep the policy confidential to top management so that competitors cannot copy it
B. Replace the policy with a consolidated list of all post orders and site instructions so that every guard knows his exact duties at each post
C. Add commitments to meet applicable legal and other requirements, respect human rights and continually improve
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 5.2
The policy must provide a framework for objectives and include commitments to satisfy applicable requirements (including legal requirements and human rights obligations) and to continual improvement; it must also be communicated within the organization and available to interested parties as appropriate. Post orders are operational documents, not a policy.
8. In a PSC, no one has been given responsibility for reporting on SOMS performance to top management, and each department head assumes someone else will do it. Which requirement is NOT met?
A. Establishing a documented process for evaluating compliance with applicable legal and other requirements at planned intervals
B. Assigning responsibility and authority for SOMS conformity and for reporting on its performance

Answer: B · ISO 18788: 5.3
Clause 5.3 requires top management to assign and communicate responsibilities and authorities for ensuring that the SOMS conforms to the standard and for reporting on SOMS performance to top management. The other options relate to 4.1 and 9.1.2, which are not the issue here.
Scenario 1 – Nahr Shield Security (starting the SOMS project)

Nahr Shield Security (NSS) is a fictional private security company based in Basra with about 600 personnel. It provides static guarding at two oil-field camps for an international oil company (IOC) and armed convoy escort between the camps and Umm Qasr port. NSS also hires vehicles from a local transport firm and, during peak periods, uses guards supplied by a smaller local guard company. The IOC has told NSS that ISO 18788 certification will be a condition for renewing its contract in 18 months. The CEO appointed the Operations Director as SOMS project lead.

In the first project meeting the following was proposed: (1) limit the SOMS scope to static guarding only, because convoy escort "is high-risk and might produce audit findings"; (2) the stakeholder list should include the IOC, the Ministry of Interior and NSS employees only; tribal communities living along the convoy routes were left out "because they are not our clients"; (3) summer heat above 50 °C has caused several cases of heat exhaustion among guards and two flash floods closed convoy routes last winter, but the team agreed that "climate is not a security topic".

9. Is the proposal to exclude convoy escort from the SOMS scope acceptable?
A. Yes, provided the exclusion is mentioned in the IOC contract rather than in the scope statement
B. Yes; the organization is free to exclude any activity that might fail a certification audit
C. No; excluding a core activity to avoid audit findings is not a valid basis for setting the scope
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 4.3
Under 4.3, the scope must be determined considering the issues, stakeholder requirements and activities of the organization. Excluding a high-risk core service to avoid scrutiny would misrepresent the SOMS to the client, which explicitly needs the certified system to cover its services.
10. What should NSS do about the tribal communities living along the convoy routes?
A. Identify them as interested parties and determine their relevant needs and expectations
B. Consider them only if they file a formal written complaint with the Ministry of Interior or with the IOC
C. Leave them out, since only contractual stakeholders need to be considered in the SOMS
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 4.2
Clause 4.2 requires the organization to determine the interested parties relevant to the SOMS and their requirements; communities affected by security operations are key interested parties, especially for human rights impacts. Being a client is not the criterion.
11. How should NSS treat the heat exhaustion cases and flash floods in the light of ISO 18788:2015/Amd 1:2024?
A. Record the floods and heat only as natural disasters, which fall outside the scope of any security operations management system
B. Determine whether climate change is a relevant issue and, as it affects operations, address it in context and risk
C. Ignore the issue, because the amendment applies only to companies emitting large amounts of greenhouse gases
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 4.1; 4.2 (Amd 1:2024)
Amendment 1:2024 added to 4.1 that the organization shall determine whether climate change is a relevant issue, and a note to 4.2 that relevant interested parties can have climate-related requirements. Here climate effects clearly influence guard welfare and route safety.
12. Before writing new procedures, the project lead wants to understand which ISO 18788 requirements NSS already meets. What is the most appropriate first step?
A. Carry out a gap analysis of current practices against ISO 18788
B. Book the Stage 1 audit, so that the certification body identifies the gaps for NSS
C. Purchase a full set of template procedures and replace all existing documents with them
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: Annex C
Annex C of ISO 18788 describes getting started with a gap analysis. It shows what exists, what is missing and where to prioritize. Templates ignore existing practices, and a certification body may not act as a consultant.
13. Regarding the vehicle hire firm and the local guard company, which ISO 18788 requirement is most relevant?
A. Uniforms and markings, since subcontracted guards may wear uniforms different from NSS guards
B. Management review inputs, since subcontractors are only discussed at management level
C. Supply chain and subcontractor mapping and analysis, including the related risks
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 4.1.4
Clause 4.1.4 requires the organization to map and analyse its supply chain and subcontractors and the related risks, so that outsourced activities meet the requirements of the SOMS. The other clauses do not address this directly.
14. The board of a PSC asks the SOMS project lead for a business case before approving the implementation project. What should the business case mainly contain?
A. Only the certification body's quotation and audit-day estimate, since obtaining the certificate is the sole purpose of the project
B. The complete set of operating procedures, so that the board can approve each one in advance
C. Expected benefits such as contract eligibility and risk reduction, with estimated costs, resources and timeline
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 5.1; 7.1 (implementation planning)
A business case justifies the project by setting the expected benefits (client requirements, reduced legal and human rights risk, operational improvement) against costs, resources and schedule. Detailed procedures come later, and the certificate is not the only benefit.
15. A PSC provides static guarding in Basra and Kirkuk and close protection in Kuwait. When defining the SOMS scope, what must the PSC consider?
A. Only the services provided under the contract that is closest to its renewal date
B. Its issues, stakeholder requirements, and the services, sites and legal environments involved
C. Only the locations where a government authority has formally requested ISO 18788 certification
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 4.3
Clause 4.3 requires the boundaries and applicability of the SOMS to be determined considering the issues in 4.1, the requirements in 4.2, and the organization's activities and locations; the scope must be documented. Different countries bring different legal requirements that must be reflected.
16. A PSC sets the objective "zero unlawful use of force incidents in 2027". According to ISO 18788, what must the plan to achieve this objective determine?
A. The insurance premium the PSC will pay if an unlawful use of force incident occurs
B. What will be done, resources, who is responsible, when it will be completed and how results are evaluated
C. Which guards and supervisors will be dismissed or disciplined if the objective has not been achieved by the end of the year
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 6.2
When planning how to achieve objectives, the organization must determine what will be done, the resources required, who will be responsible, when it will be completed and how results will be evaluated. Disciplinary consequences and insurance are not the planning elements the standard requires.
17. A PSC is selecting treatment options for the risks of a new convoy route that passes close to several villages. With whom should it communicate and consult before finalising the treatment plan?
A. Relevant internal and external stakeholders, such as the client, its personnel and community representatives
B. Nobody outside the operations department, since all route information is always strictly confidential and must never be shared
C. Only the insurance broker, because the main purpose of risk treatment is to reduce premiums
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 6.1.3
Clause 6.1.3 requires internal and external risk communication and consultation with relevant stakeholders. Sensitive details can be protected, but consulting the client, personnel and affected communities improves the quality of treatment decisions and reduces human rights impacts.
18. During planning, a consultant reminds a PSC that ISO 18788 includes "structural requirements" under resources. Which evidence would best show these requirements are addressed?
A. Proof of legal registration and licensing, a documented structure, insurance and financial capacity
B. A list of the PSC's vehicles and weapons sorted by purchase date and by location
C. A marketing brochure describing the PSC's history and its most important clients over the years
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 7.1.2
Clause 7.1.2 deals with the structural basis of the organization: a defined and documented structure, legal status and licensing, and sufficient financial and insurance capacity to meet its obligations, including liabilities towards people harmed. A brochure or asset list does not show this.
Scenario 2 – Delta Line Security (risk assessment for convoy operations)

Delta Line Security (DLS), a fictional PSC, escorts trucks carrying drilling equipment from Umm Qasr to oil fields north of Basra. Its current risk register lists only risks to the client's cargo and to DLS vehicles and weapons. No risk criteria have been defined: each convoy commander rates risks as "high", "medium" or "low" according to personal judgement, so the same threat is rated differently by different teams.

The client has asked DLS to use a new, shorter route that passes through the centre of a busy market town. DLS intelligence reports that a roadside IED was recently found on another route used by local contractors. The Ministry of Interior has also changed the conditions attached to PSC weapons and vehicle permits, and DLS learnt about it only when a convoy was stopped at a checkpoint.

19. What is the main weakness of the DLS risk register?
A. It ignores risks to stakeholders, including human rights impacts on local people
B. It contains too many risks, which makes it difficult for convoy commanders to read
C. It is kept by the operations department instead of by the finance department
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 6.1.1
ISO 18788 risk assessment (6.1.1) must consider risks to the organization and to internal and external stakeholders, including impacts on human rights and fundamental freedoms. A register limited to cargo and company assets misses the key purpose of the standard.
20. What should DLS do first to make risk ratings consistent across convoy teams?
A. Let the most experienced convoy commander re-rate all risks using his own judgement
B. Ask the client to provide its own risk ratings for every route that DLS uses
C. Define and document risk criteria, with likelihood and consequence scales and acceptance levels
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 4.1.5
Clause 4.1.5 requires the organization to define the criteria used to evaluate the significance of risk. Without shared criteria, risk ratings depend on individual opinion. The client's ratings may be an input but do not replace DLS's own criteria.
21. Which of the following is an example of risk AVOIDANCE for the market town route?
A. Adding a paramedic and an extra escort vehicle to each convoy on the route
B. Using an alternative route that bypasses the town centre
C. Buying additional third-party liability insurance covering the route
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 6.1.1; 6.2
Avoidance removes the risk by not undertaking the risky activity (driving through the town). Adding a paramedic or vehicle modifies the consequence or likelihood; insurance shares the financial consequence. All are treatment options considered under 6.1.
22. How should DLS handle the intelligence about the IED found on another route?
A. Publish it on social media to warn the general public about the threat
B. Keep it within the intelligence cell to avoid alarming drivers and the client, unless the threat is later confirmed on DLS routes
C. Share it with the client and relevant personnel through its risk communication process and review the route
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 6.1.3; 7.4.3
Clauses 6.1.3 and 7.4.3 require risk information to be communicated to relevant internal and external stakeholders, with appropriate protection of sensitive information. Withholding it endangers people; public release may compromise operations.
23. Which control would best have prevented DLS from being surprised by the change in permit conditions?
A. A register of legal requirements with an owner who monitors and communicates changes
B. Keeping a copy of the original permits in every convoy vehicle at all times
C. Asking convoy commanders to negotiate with checkpoint officers whenever permits are questioned
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 6.1.2
Clause 6.1.2 requires the organization to identify and have access to applicable legal and other requirements, keep them up to date and determine how they apply. A register with an owner who monitors changes addresses the root problem.
24. A PSC's convoy route crosses a 40 km stretch where VHF radios have no coverage, and convoys have lost contact with the operations room several times. What is the most appropriate action?
A. Provide redundant communication means, such as satellite phones and trackers, and test them before each move
B. Record the problem in the risk register and accept it, as coverage is the network provider's responsibility
C. Instruct convoys to stop and wait at the start of the dead zone until radio contact with the operations room has been fully restored
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 7.4.2
Clause 7.4.2 requires reliable operational communications, including redundancy where needed and testing of equipment. Accepting the gap leaves convoys without command and emergency support; waiting at the dead zone increases exposure without solving the problem.
25. During a site visit, the SOMS manager finds three different versions of the rules for the use of force card carried by guards at the same camp. What is the most appropriate action?
A. Allow each guard to keep the version he has learnt best, since the core message is similar in all three
B. Withdraw obsolete versions, control distribution of the current one and brief guards on the changes
C. Collect all cards and stop issuing them, because such rules should only be explained verbally
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 7.5.3
Clause 7.5.3 requires documented information to be available where needed, and obsolete versions to be prevented from unintended use (distribution, access, version control). Multiple versions of rules for the use of force can lead to unlawful or inconsistent use of force.
26. A supervisor discovers that gate guards accept small cash "gifts" from truck drivers, who then pass without a full vehicle search. Beyond dealing with the guards involved, what is the most appropriate SOMS response?
A. Reinforce the code of ethical conduct, including anti-bribery rules, through training, supervision and discipline
B. Remove vehicle searches from the post orders so that there is no longer any opportunity for bribery, and rely on the client's own checks
C. Increase guards' salaries and allow small gifts below an agreed value, as this is local custom
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.2
Clause 8.2 requires norms of behaviour and a code of ethical conduct, communicated and enforced. Allowing gifts that bypass security controls undermines both ethics and security; removing searches would cut a necessary control.
27. At a camp gate, female visitors are being body-searched by male guards because no female guards are on the roster. What should the PSC do?
A. Ensure searches respect dignity and cultural norms, using female searchers or alternative screening
B. Continue the current practice, since searches are a client requirement and must not be delayed
C. Stop searching all female visitors so as to avoid any complaint from the local community
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.4.2
Clause 8.4.2 requires searches to be carried out lawfully and with respect for dignity, including gender and cultural considerations. Stopping searches for one group creates a security gap; continuing male searches of women is likely to violate dignity and local norms.
28. A client asks the PSC to have its armed guards at a checkpoint wear plain civilian clothes to look "less threatening". What is the most appropriate response?
A. Explain that guards must stay identifiable and distinguishable from public forces, as law and contract require
B. Agree, because looking less threatening to the local population is always the most important objective at a vehicle checkpoint
C. Agree, but let each guard choose his own clothes so that the team does not look uniformed
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.6.4
Clause 8.6.4 addresses uniforms and markings: personnel must be identifiable and distinguishable from military and police forces, consistent with applicable law and contract. Armed personnel in plain clothes cannot easily be identified or held accountable.
29. During July and August, temperatures at desert guard posts exceed 50 °C and several guards have collapsed. What should the PSC implement first?
A. A formal request to the client to suspend all guarding services at desert posts during the hottest summer months of the year
B. A disciplinary rule for guards who leave their posts during the hottest part of the day
C. A heat stress plan with work-rest cycles, shade, water and supervisor checks, reflected in the risk assessment
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.7; 4.1 (Amd 1:2024)
Clause 8.7 requires occupational health and safety to be managed for persons working on behalf of the organization. Extreme heat, increasingly linked to climate change (Amd 1:2024 to 4.1), should be addressed through risk assessment and practical controls, not discipline or suspending services.
Scenario 3 – Basra Gateway Guarding (static site, use of force and weapons)

Basra Gateway Guarding (BGG), a fictional PSC, protects an IOC accommodation camp. Armed guards with rifles control the main gate; unarmed guards work inside the camp. Last month a car approached the gate at speed and a guard fired two warning shots; the car stopped and the driver turned out to be a lost delivery driver. BGG guards carry no less-lethal equipment and have never practised de-escalation.

An internal check found that: rifles are issued from the armoury without any signature, and ammunition is counted only weekly; one guard who failed his annual weapons requalification test is still on the armed roster because "he is experienced". Last week about 30 unemployed young men from a nearby village blocked the gate demanding jobs. The shift supervisor seized two of them and held them in an empty container for six hours until the client's camp manager arrived.

30. Which requirement should BGG primarily address following the warning shots incident?
A. A graduated use-of-force continuum with de-escalation and less-lethal options, backed by training
B. Increasing the number of armed guards at the gate so that warning shots are no longer needed
C. Moving the gate further back from the road so that approaching drivers have more time to stop
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.3.3; 8.3.4; 8.3.7
Clause 8.3.3 requires a use-of-force continuum so that force is the minimum necessary and proportionate, and 8.3.4 covers less-lethal force. Physical measures may help, but the core weakness is the absence of graduated options and training (8.3.7).
31. Keeping the guard who failed requalification on the armed roster violates which requirement most directly?
A. Communicating the whistle-blower policy to all personnel working at the camp
B. Uniforms and markings, which require armed guards to be clearly identifiable to the public
C. Weapons authorization: armed personnel must be trained and qualified on the weapons they carry
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.3.2
Clause 8.3.2 requires that only personnel who are authorized, trained and qualified on the specific weapon carry it. Experience does not replace current qualification.
32. Which control would best correct the armoury weakness?
A. Locking the armoury at night and issuing weapons freely to any guard during the day shift
B. Signed issue and return by serial number, restricted access and daily ammunition reconciliation
C. Asking each guard to keep his rifle at home in order to reduce armoury traffic
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 8.6.3
Clause 8.6.3 requires weapons and munitions to be controlled, stored securely and accounted for, with records. Serial-number logs, signatures, restricted access and frequent reconciliation provide traceability.
33. How should the supervisor have acted regarding the two protesters he seized?
A. Hold them in a secure place until the client's camp manager arrives and decides what should be done with them
B. Release them only after they sign a written promise never to return to the camp gate
C. Apprehend only if lawful and necessary, treat them humanely and hand them to the authorities without delay
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.4.1; 8.1.3
Clause 8.4.1 allows apprehension only where lawful and necessary (e.g. to protect people), and requires humane treatment and transfer to the competent public authorities at the earliest opportunity. A six-hour detention in a container awaiting the client is not acceptable.
34. What is the best longer-term action to reduce the risk of similar protests escalating at the gate?
A. Asking the police to arrest anyone who gathers near the camp gate in future
B. An accessible community grievance mechanism, communicated locally and coordinated with the client's liaison
C. Installing higher fences and posting more armed guards facing the direction of the village
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 7.4.4; 8.8.3
Clauses 7.4.4 and 8.8.3 require complaint and grievance procedures that are communicated to and accessible by external stakeholders. Giving the community a legitimate channel reduces escalation; more force does not address the cause.
35. A guard wants to report that his supervisor sells company fuel, but fears losing his job. Which SOMS element is most directly designed for this situation?
A. The internal audit programme, which will eventually detect the fuel theft through sampling
B. A whistle-blower policy with confidential channels and protection from retaliation
C. The management review, at which the guard can present his concerns directly to the CEO
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 8.8.4; 7.4.5
Clauses 8.8.4 and 7.4.5 require a whistle-blower policy that is communicated to personnel and protects those who report in good faith from retaliation. Audits and management reviews are not confidential reporting channels.
36. A farmer complains to a PSC that one of its convoy vehicles damaged his irrigation channel. What is the most appropriate way to handle this complaint?
A. Refer the farmer to the client, because the convoy was carrying the client's cargo at the time
B. Reject the complaint unless the farmer can provide photographs taken at the moment of the damage
C. Record it, investigate fairly, respond in good time with any remedy and keep records of the outcome
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.8.3
Clause 8.8.3 requires procedures for internal and external complaints and grievances that are accessible and fair, including investigation, response and remedy where appropriate, with records retained. Rejecting the complaint or redirecting it to the client avoids the PSC's own accountability.
37. A PSC has no plan for evacuating client personnel from a camp if it comes under armed attack. Which requirement should the PSC address first?
A. Communicating the whistle-blower policy to all client personnel staying at the camp
B. Uniforms and markings, so that client personnel can recognise PSC staff during an evacuation
C. Prevention and management of undesirable or disruptive events, with response plans and roles
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.1.4
Clause 8.1.4 requires the organization to plan for the prevention and management of undesirable or disruptive events, including emergency response and evacuation, with assigned roles. The plan must later be exercised (9.1.3).
38. The local police commander asks a PSC to lend six of its armed guards to help man a public checkpoint outside the client's site for a week. What should the PSC do?
A. Decline unless the support is lawful, authorised by contract and covered by clear rules for the use of force
B. Accept, because supporting national law enforcement always takes priority over any contractual or internal company limits
C. Accept, provided the guards wear police uniforms so that the public sees them as police officers
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.5.1; 8.6.4
Clause 8.5.1 allows support to law enforcement only where it is legally permitted and contractually authorized, with defined rules for the use of force and command arrangements. Wearing police uniforms would breach the requirement to be distinguishable from public forces (8.6.4).
39. At night, a guard sees a man running away from the camp fence with a stolen roll of copper cable. Nobody is threatened. May the guard use lethal force to stop him?
A. Yes, because protecting client property is the main purpose of an armed guarding contract
B. Yes, if the guard has first shouted a warning in Arabic and English and fired a warning shot
C. No; lethal force is only for an imminent threat of death or serious injury to self or others
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.3.5
Clause 8.3.5 restricts lethal force to self-defence or defence of others against an imminent threat of death or serious injury. Theft of property alone never justifies lethal force; warnings do not change that.
40. To reduce costs, a PSC's logistics officer proposes buying ammunition from an unlicensed dealer in a local market. What is the most appropriate response?
A. Reject it; weapons and munitions must come from lawful, licensed sources with full records
B. Accept it for training ammunition only, while operational ammunition still comes from licensed sources
C. Accept it, provided the ammunition is tested on the range before being issued to guards
Show answer
Answer: A · ISO 18788: 8.6.3
Clause 8.6.3 requires the lawful procurement, storage, control and accounting of weapons, hazardous materials and munitions. Buying from unlicensed sources is unlawful and undermines traceability, whatever the use.
Scenario 4 – Southern Star Protection (personnel, training and subcontracting)

Southern Star Protection (SSP), a fictional PSC, won a new static-guarding contract and had to recruit 120 local guards in three weeks. Background screening consisted only of a police certificate of no criminal record; HR later discovered that several recruits had served in an armed group that has been accused of abuses. Induction training lasts two days, covers weapons handling and post orders, and is delivered from English slides, although most guards speak only Arabic. There is no training on human rights, the code of conduct, or the grievance mechanism.

To fill the remaining posts, SSP subcontracted 40 guards from a local company; SSP has never seen that company's screening or training records. Training attendance and test results are kept as photos on each supervisor's mobile phone.

41. What is the most appropriate improvement to SSP's background screening?
A. Accepting the police certificate alone, since it is an official document issued by the government
B. Asking each recruit to sign a declaration that he has never been involved in any abuses
C. Multi-source vetting of employment and service history, references and any link to human rights abuses
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 8.6.2
Clause 8.6.2 requires screening of personnel appropriate to the role, which in this context includes previous service, references and checks for past human rights abuses. A police certificate or self-declaration alone does not reveal membership of armed groups accused of abuses.
42. Delivering induction training using English slides to Arabic-speaking guards fails which requirement?
A. Determining the external and internal issues relevant to the purpose of the SOMS
B. Ensuring competence through effective training and evaluating whether it was achieved
C. Maintaining a statement of conformance signed and communicated by top management
Show answer
Answer: B · ISO 18788: 7.2
Clauses 7.2.1 and 7.2.3 require the organization to ensure competence through appropriate training and to evaluate its effectiveness. Training in a language trainees do not understand cannot deliver competence.
43. Which content must be added to the induction programme as a priority?
A. Company history, the CEO's biography and the full organization chart of SSP
B. Advanced driving and vehicle recovery techniques for armoured vehicles, delivered to all static guards
C. Human rights, the code of conduct, rules for the use of force and the grievance mechanism
Show answer
Answer: C · ISO 18788: 7.2; 7.3; 8.1.3
Clauses 7.2, 7.3 and 8.1.3 require personnel to be aware of the policy, their contribution, human rights obligations, the code of conduct and procedures such as grievance and reporting. Driving training is role-specific and not a priority for static guards.

C. Send the plan to the hospital for approval and consider it tested

Answer: A · ISO 18788: 9.1.3
Clause 9.1.3 requires plans to be exercised and tested, with documented results used to improve them. Approval on paper is not a test.
